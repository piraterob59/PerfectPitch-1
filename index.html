        Settings

        How close (in cents — 100ths of a semitone) your sung pitch needs to land to the original vocal's pitch to color green and count as a full-credit hit. Notes within 50 cents color yellow and count as half credit; anything further is red and counts as a miss. Lower is stricter.

        About

        PerfectPitch separates vocals from instrumentals using LALAL.AI. Since LALAL.AI's API blocks direct calls from a browser, imports go through a small proxy (see worker/README.md in the project repo) that holds the LALAL.AI license key server-side — nothing secret is stored on this device or in this app's code.

        Everything else — your song library, separated stems, and pitch data — is stored only on this device, in this browser's local database.
